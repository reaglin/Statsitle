**Fundamental Statistics for Engineers** follows the course EGN3443, *Probability and Statistics for Engineers*, topic by topic: describing data, probability and its distributions, sampling, estimation, hypothesis tests, regression and the analysis of variance. Each module builds on the one before it, so take them in order. Every Example can be opened in the Workbench, and every Exercise is checked with answers the Workbench calculates.
