**Additional Applications of Statistics** shows how the statistics of Part I are used in other fields, with each field's own questions, vocabulary and data: **Biology and Medicine** (clinical trials, epidemiology, diagnostic tests, survival, genetics, dose–response), **Business and Economics** (econometrics, forecasting, monetary policy, risk, asset pricing, market research), **Environmental and Earth Sciences** (floods, climate trends, water quality, spatial statistics, earthquakes, ecology), **Social Sciences and Psychology** (polls, scales, experiments, causation, psychometrics, the replication crisis), **Data Science and Computing** (model evaluation, classifiers, online experiments, naive Bayes, anomalies, system performance) and **Sports** (sabermetrics, the Pythagorean expectation, ratings, the hot hand, odds, sports science).

The modules do not teach the methods again: each page points to the Part I item that does. Where a method belongs to Part II (still in development), the page explains only as much as the application needs. Each module stands alone: start with the field you need. Most of the data are illustrative, made up for this book and labelled as such; the few real data sets are cited.
