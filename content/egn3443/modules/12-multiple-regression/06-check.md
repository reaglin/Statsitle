# Module check: multiple regression

This check covers the five items of Module 12. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- building a multiple regression equation: its coefficients, degrees of freedom and predictions;
- interpreting partial regression coefficients, with the other predictors held fixed;
- telling R² from adjusted R², and calculating adjusted R²;
- the overall F-test: its hypotheses and its statistic;
- justifying the inclusion or removal of a predictor.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
