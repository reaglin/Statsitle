# Module check: ANOVA

This check covers the six items of Module 14. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- why one ANOVA instead of many t-tests (the familywise error rate);
- checking independence, normality and equal variances, and what to do when they fail;
- building the one-way ANOVA table and its F;
- telling main effects from an interaction in a two-way design;
- choosing a post-hoc procedure and reading its output.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench to check your work. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete, and with it the course's path. The Workbench stays for your own data: choose any analysis of the course in its list (Ctrl+B).
