# Module check: sampling distributions

This check covers the five items of Module 6. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- telling population parameters from sample statistics;
- calculating the mean and standard error of x̄, and a probability about x̄;
- predicting how the sample size changes the standard error, and the n a target needs;
- deciding when the Central Limit Theorem lets x̄ be treated as normal;
- telling ordinary sampling variability from a real change.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Sampling distribution**) to check your hand calculations. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for; a sample size is a whole number.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
