Welcome to Statistics+. It follows EGN3443, *Probability and Statistics for Engineers*, topic by topic: descriptive statistics, probability, distributions, sampling, confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, regression and ANOVA. Each topic is explained, worked on an engineering dataset, and then given to you to do.

This short module is the course's Week 1. It has three items:

- **How Statistics+ works**: the list on the left, the four tabs of every item, the Workbench, and how your progress is kept.
- **Python and R for this course**: where to run the code, and how to check that it gives the same numbers as Statistics+.
- **The report template**: the sections every assignment in the course is written in.

The three outcomes above are this program's version of the course's Week 1 outcomes, which are about finding your way around the course website.

Press the button above (**Start with 0.1 How Statistics+ works**) to begin. Inside an item, **Next ▸** (top right) goes on to the next one.
