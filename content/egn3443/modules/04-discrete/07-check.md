# Module check: discrete probability distributions

This check covers the six items of Module 4. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- building a valid probability distribution (non-negative, adding to 1);
- calculating the expected value and variance from a PMF;
- telling a binomial situation from a Poisson one;
- calculating exact binomial and Poisson probabilities with the right parameters;
- interpreting probabilities and expected values for a decision.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Discrete distribution**) to check your hand calculations. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
