# Module check: business and economics

This check covers the six items of Module 20. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all six outcomes of the module:

- reading an elasticity from a log–log regression, and spotting an omitted variable;
- forecasting with a trend, a moving average or exponential smoothing, with the forecast's uncertainty;
- calculating inflation from a price index, and reading a Taylor rule;
- the expected value, the risk and the Value at Risk of a decision;
- beta, alpha and diversification;
- a survey's margin of error and an A/B test of two conversion rates.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench to check your work. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
