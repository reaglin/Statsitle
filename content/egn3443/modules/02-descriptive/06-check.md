# Module check: descriptive statistics

This check covers the five items of Module 2. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- calculating the mean, median and mode (questions with a small dataset to work by hand);
- calculating the range, variance and standard deviation;
- classifying the shape of a distribution;
- choosing the measure of center for a dataset;
- interpreting the numbers for a reader.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench to check your hand calculations. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
