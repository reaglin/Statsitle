# Module check: point estimation and confidence intervals

This check covers the seven items of Module 7. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- telling unbiasedness from efficiency when choosing an estimator;
- using the central limit theorem and the standard error σ/√n;
- building intervals for a mean and for a proportion, with the right choice of z or t;
- judging how the confidence level, the sample size and the variability change the width, and finding the n for a margin of error;
- interpreting an interval in context, in correct words.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Confidence interval**, **Sample size**) to check your hand calculations. The critical values you need are in the questions or in the tables of items 7.3 and 7.6. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
