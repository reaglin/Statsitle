# Module check: statistics for engineers

This check covers the six items of Module 1. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, two for each of the module's five outcomes:

- calculating descriptive statistics for a small dataset, by hand;
- choosing the normal, binomial or Poisson distribution for a scenario;
- interpreting a confidence interval and a hypothesis test against a tolerance or a decision;
- reading a box plot's fences and a histogram's shape;
- telling common-cause from special-cause variation, and what spread means for quality.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench to check your hand calculations. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
