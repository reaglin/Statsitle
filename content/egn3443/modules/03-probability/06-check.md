# Module check: probability concepts

This check covers the five items of Module 3. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- building a sample space with a list, a table or a tree;
- calculating probabilities with the complement, addition and multiplication rules;
- comparing theoretical and experimental probability, with the course's **three-card 21** simulation, and the Law of Large Numbers;
- saying what a probability means for an engineering decision;
- choosing the right rule for a multi-step problem.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Probability rules** and **Simulation**) to check your hand calculations. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
