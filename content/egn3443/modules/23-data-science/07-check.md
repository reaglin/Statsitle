# Module check: data science and computing

This check covers the six items of Module 23. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all six outcomes of the module:

- training and test error, and data leakage;
- precision and recall from a confusion matrix;
- false winners among many metrics;
- naive Bayes in odds form;
- k-means and the box-plot fences;
- the availability of redundant systems.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench to check your work. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
