# Module check: continuous probability distributions

This check covers the six items of Module 5. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all four outcomes of the module:

- the density and the cumulative distribution function: probabilities as areas;
- probabilities and percentiles for the uniform, normal and exponential distributions, with z-scores and the memoryless property;
- choosing the model that suits a quantity, and judging a fit to data;
- interpreting a probability for a decision.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Continuous distribution**) to check your hand calculations. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
