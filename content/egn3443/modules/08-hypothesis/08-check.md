# Module check: hypothesis testing

This check covers the seven items of Module 8. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- writing H₀ and H₁ from an engineering claim, with the right tail;
- choosing the test for a study: one-sample, paired or two-sample t, chi-square, or ANOVA;
- calculating a t statistic, a p-value and a chi-square statistic;
- deciding by comparing p with α, and telling statistical from practical significance;
- telling a Type I error from a Type II error, and what each costs.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**One-sample test** and **Chi-square tests**) to check your hand calculations. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again", and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
