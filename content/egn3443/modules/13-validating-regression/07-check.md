# Module check: validating regression

This check covers the six items of Module 13. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module. Like the course's *Fit for Purpose* activity, most of them hand you a fitted model, its plots or its numbers, and ask what you would conclude or do:

- reading residual plots for curvature, unequal spread and dependence;
- computing residuals and what they add up to, and reading their signs;
- choosing between the holdout method, k-fold and leave-one-out cross-validation, and reading their results;
- diagnosing multicollinearity, outliers and influential observations;
- eliminating weak candidate models with a reason, and choosing the refinement and the final model.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Simple linear regression**, **Multiple regression**) to check your work. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
