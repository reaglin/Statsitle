# Module check: regression analysis

This check covers the six items of Module 11. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- building the least-squares line and predicting with it, inside the range of the data;
- reading regression output: the slope and its interval, R², the F statistic, p-values, and which interval to use for a prediction;
- telling when a question needs a multiple regression rather than a simple one;
- judging the assumptions from residual plots: linearity, independence, normality and equal variance;
- writing a regression result for a reader who is not an engineer.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Simple linear regression**) to check your work. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
