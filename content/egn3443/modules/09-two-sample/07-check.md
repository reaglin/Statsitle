# Module check: hypothesis testing II

This check covers the six items of Module 9. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, over all five outcomes of the module:

- choosing the two-sample z-test, a two-sample t-test or the paired t-test for a situation;
- calculating the test statistic and its degrees of freedom;
- judging whether the assumptions (independence, normality, equal variances) hold;
- reading p-values and confidence intervals, and telling statistical from practical significance;
- choosing the non-parametric test when the t-test's assumptions fail.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Two-sample test**) to check your work. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
