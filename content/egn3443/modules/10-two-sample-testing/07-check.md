# Module check: two-sample hypothesis testing

This check covers the six items of Module 10. Each item is complete when you pass its exercise or its questions.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab, two for each of the module's five outcomes:

- telling independent samples from paired ones;
- checking the assumptions and conditions of a test before using it;
- calculating the pooled and the paired t statistic;
- calculating the pooled proportion and the two-proportion z statistic;
- interpreting p-values and confidence intervals in the context of a decision.

Have a calculator, or Python or R, ready. You may use the Workbench (**Two-sample test**) to check your work. Type numbers with a decimal point, rounded to the number of decimals the question asks for.

You need 80 % (8 of 10) to pass, and you can try again. A wrong answer says "look again" and names the item that teaches it, and the explanation shows once your answer is right.

When every item and the check are complete, the module is complete.
