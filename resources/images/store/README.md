## Which file goes in which Partner Center slot

Partner Center → *Store listings → English (United States) → Store logos*.

| File | Slot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `StoreLogo-300x300.png` | Store logo (300 × 300) | The mark, not the logo — this is shown small in search results, where the wordmark and the percentages are unreadable |
| `BoxArt-1080x1080.png` | 1:1 box art (1080 × 1080) | The logo, corners filled to its own navy |
| `PosterArt-720x1080.png` | 2:3 poster art (720 × 1080) | The logo centred on that navy |
| `SuperHeroArt-2400x1200.png` | Super hero art (2400 × 1200) | Logo left, tagline right |
| `HeroImage-1920x1080.png` | 16:9 hero / featured image (1920 × 1080) | Same composition at 16:9 |
| `asset-contact-sheet.png` | — | Not for upload. A proof sheet of the MSIX tiles at their real pixel sizes, for eyeballing after a regeneration |

**Screenshots are not generated here.** Partner Center wants .png, at least 1366 × 768, up to
ten. The shot list is at the end of `resources/STORE-LISTING.md` — and capture wider than 1366,
because SMADA's first set came out 1345 px and had to be padded.

## The two visual layers, and why

The logo is a wordmark over an annotated bell curve. It reads beautifully large and turns to mush
small: by 44 px "statistle" is a grey smear and "68.2%" is noise.

So the assets split by size:

- **The mark** — the logo's bell curve redrawn without the wordmark or the annotations, in the
  logo's own palette (`#142E4F` ground, `#01A9E6` centre band, `#07C05A`, `#E38706`, white
  outline). It carries the 44 × 44, 71 × 71 and 150 × 150 tiles, the taskbar target sizes, the
  `.statistle` file icon, the 300 × 300 store logo and `Statistle.ico`. Legible at 16 px.
- **The logo** — carries the 310 × 310 tile, the splash screen, and the box, poster and hero
  images. Its white rounded-rectangle surround is flood-filled to the navy so tiles are
  full-bleed rather than showing a white notch in each corner.

The wide tile is neither: cropped to 310 × 150 the logo keeps either the wordmark or the curve
but never both, so that tile is the mark's glyph beside the name.

## The size cap

WACK fails any logo image in the package over **204800 bytes**. `Write-Capped` in the generator
writes each package asset at 256 colours and steps down through 128, 64 and 32 until it fits.
On flat artwork this is invisible — the 1240 px tile lands at 32 colours and is
indistinguishable from 24-bit. The listing images above are not in the package, are not capped,
and stay 24-bit.
