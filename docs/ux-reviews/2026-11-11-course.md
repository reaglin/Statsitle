screens: `Learning/CoursePage.cs`, `TaskWorkspace.cs`, `LearnerScreen`, `LearnerDialogs`, `QuizView`, the Course |
Workbench strip, and the Module 2 pilot as a student meets it. It also looked at pictures of the rendered screens
