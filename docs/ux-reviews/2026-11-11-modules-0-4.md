Plan 11.2–11.5. Inline review by the `ux-reviewer` agent (cognitive walkthrough + Nielsen heuristics) of the new
content as a first-year student meets it, compared with the approved Module 2 pilot, and of the two new behaviours:
the mistake an exercise recognizes and figures in quiz questions. Result: 0 Critical, 7 Major, 11 Minor. All were
fixed the same day; the visual checks at the end are left for the hand-test.
